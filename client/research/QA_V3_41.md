## Limits
Template detection is evidence-based and conservative, not proof that every possible template will be detected. Ambiguous testimonials need verification. Translation/consolidation failures retain original records and are recorded in limits; no identities are confirmed by name alone. Customer snapshots, browser downloads and credentials are not committed.
